Our story

Born from a dream, built by a family.

Misión Mochila was founded by Rosa “Rosie” Romero. She was raised in Houston by Salvadoran parents, and on visits to El Salvador she saw how many children went without. Her parents and her aunt always packed suitcases full of gifts for families in need. Rosie wanted to do more.

In 2024, Rosie and her husband Roberto Romero, who was born in El Salvador, turned that dream into action. In 2025 Misión Mochila became a registered 501(c)(3), and Verónica Franco, born and raised in El Salvador and part of the mission from day one, joined as Vice President.

“More than items, we deliver hope, love, and the reminder that every child matters.”

Rosie Romero · Founder Roberto Romero · Co-founder Verónica Franco · Vice President

Mission

To deliver backpacks filled with school supplies, clothing, hygiene products and toys to children in El Salvador, while sharing the love and hope of God.

Vision

A world where every child feels equipped, encouraged and empowered to chase their dreams, no matter where they come from.

What's inside a mochila

Everything a child needs to start the school year proud.

Each backpack is packed by volunteers in Houston and hand-delivered in El Salvador. Every item is new, so every child gets something that is truly their own.

Why we deliver at year's end

We deliver before the holidays, on purpose.

A fresh start

The school year in El Salvador begins in January. A backpack delivered before then means every child walks in on day one prepared and confident.

A Christmas outfit

The new clothes become their holiday best, something special to wear during the most meaningful time of the year.

A toy to open

Many parents in these communities can't afford Christmas gifts. Finding a toy inside the backpack is a moment of pure joy.

What we do

Four ways we show up for kids.

Annual Backpack Drive

The heart of Misión Mochila. Each year we collect, pack and deliver fully stocked backpacks to children in need across El Salvador.

School Adoption Program

We partner with under-resourced schools and work with their leaders to meet real needs.

  • Backpacks for every student
  • Supplies and encouragement for teachers
  • Events that make kids feel seen and celebrated

Holiday Celebrations

Distribution day is a party: music, a clown show, piñatas, face painting, treats, and a warm meal for every child and parent.

Teacher Gifts & Family Support

Teachers receive supplies and care packages, and families in extreme need receive food baskets. Supporting a child's whole circle multiplies the impact.

FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Don't see yours? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Misión Mochila is a registered 501(c)(3), so your donation may be tax-deductible. Save your receipt, and we'll provide one on request.

How much does it cost to sponsor a backpack?

Between $40 and $50 covers one fully stocked backpack.

Do I have to buy from the wish lists?

Not at all. The lists are a guide. We're happy to receive any new, age-appropriate items.

Can I donate gently used items?

We ask for new items only, so every child receives something that's just for them.

How do you choose which schools to help?

We partner with schools in vulnerable areas of El Salvador and work with local educators to identify the students most in need.

When do the backpacks get delivered?

At the end of each year, at a celebration with music, food, clowns, and gifts for the teachers too. Our 2024 and 2025 missions were in December; the 2026 mission is in November in Ozatlán.

Can I volunteer? Can my kids?

Absolutely. You can pack backpacks, help at events, write holiday letters or spread the word. Kids can write Christmas letters or draw pictures to include in the backpacks.

How can my business get involved?

We welcome corporate sponsorships, in-kind donations, and workplace toy or supply drives. Email us and we'll plan it together.

Do you only help children in El Salvador?

For now, yes. Our focus is on schools in El Salvador, and we hope to expand in the future.
